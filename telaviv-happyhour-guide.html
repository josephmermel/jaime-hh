Jaffa

בית קנדינוף

Beit Kandinof · HaTsorfim 14, Old Jaffa
🕐 Mon–Thu 6–11pm · Fri–Sat from noon
Vibe:
Quiet
An Ottoman stone-arch building at the entrance to Old Jaffa, housing a restaurant-gallery where contemporary art exhibitions rotate every three months and jazz plays in the background. The Jaffa-Mediterranean menu is seasonal and garden-sourced — think roasted fish, ceviche, French-inflected starters — served in beautiful arched rooms with warm candlelight. The most atmospheric and genuinely intimate space on the entire list. Private rooms are available for groups up to 10.

🎉 HH: ~25% off, weekday evenings

New pickNeve Tzedek

בקאפיקו

Beccafico · Shabazi 49, Neve Tzedek
🕐 Mon–Thu 5pm–midnight · Fri–Sat from 1pm
Vibe:
Quiet
Tucked into the most picturesque stretch of Neve Tzedek's main street, Beccafico is a Sicilian-Italian restaurant that has become a neighbourhood institution since 2014. Handmade pastas, wood-fired pizza, fresh fish, arancini, and an excellent wine list in a warm, unhurried interior with a back courtyard. The owner is famously present and treats every table like a guest at home. The happy hour deal (1+1 starters + 20% off all drinks) is one of the most generous in the city.

New pickCentral TLV

שילה

Shila · Montefiore 9, Central Tel Aviv
🕐 Daily from 6pm · Also weekday lunch 12:30–4pm
Vibe:
Lively
Tel Aviv's most storied seafood restaurant, just reopened in a spectacular new space: a soaring multi-room dining room in the Gymnasium Hotel with a live lobster tank, oyster station, 200-label wine room, and an open kitchen. Chef Sharon Cohen's cooking — pristine fish, impeccable pasta, generous portions — is 20+ years in the making. Specifically praised for outstanding acoustics despite the scale. The most upscale option on the list; no formal happy hour but house wine at ₪35/glass is genuinely exceptional for this level.

The sweet spot — great food, still talkable

04
Central TLV

צ'יקטי

Cicchetti · Yehuda HaLevi 58
🕐 Sun–Thu 4–10:30pm · Fri–Sat from 11am (brunch) & 6pm
Vibe:
Lively
A Venetian-style aperitivo bar on a beautiful corner of central Tel Aviv — consistently ranked among Israel's best Italian restaurants. The concept is Venetian cicchetti: small sharing plates of fresh fish, pasta, seafood, and charcuterie alongside prosecco on tap and cocktails mixed by Avi Kashi at the bar. The street tables and long bar keep it animated without being loud. The 30% aperitivo deal runs every weekday 4–6pm. Reserve ahead — this place is always full.

GF: some fish/charcuterie plates; pasta/pizza not GF DF: limited — cheese and cream central to menu
🎉 HH: 30% off everything, daily 4–6pm

ורמוטריה

Vermuteria · Nachalat Binyamin 25
🕐 Sun–Thu 5–11pm · Fri–Sat 11:30am–3pm & 6–11pm
Vibe:
Lively
The quieter, more mature sibling of Cicchetti — same ownership and chef (Michael Gertofsky), but Spanish in spirit: vermouth, sherry, pintxos, gambas al ajillo, beef tartare, pan con tomate, and a full lavraki paella. The aperitivo ritual (vermotivo) runs Sun–Thu 5–7pm. Critics note the crowd skews slightly older than Cicchetti, which for a mixed-age group is a real asset. Warm indoor room, covered outdoor seating, and street tables on lively Nachalat Binyamin.

Neve Tzedek

TYO

Shabazi 58, Neve Tzedek
🕐 Daily 12pm–midnight (Thu until 1am)
Vibe:
Lively → Loud later
A Japanese restaurant-bar in a spectacular preserved 120-year-old building in Neve Tzedek, led by Yama-San — Israel's only certified sushi master, trained at Morimoto. The menu spans traditional sushi, izakaya-style small plates, and tuna pizza that has become something of a Tel Aviv legend. The 25% happy hour (Sun–Wed 4–7pm) makes a typically premium experience accessible. Beautiful space, upscale crowd. Note: staff can be indifferent with large groups — reserve and confirm in advance.

If the mood is fun & festive — Jaffa flea market

07
Jaffa

קפטן הוקי

Captain Hook · Amiad 13, Jaffa Flea Market
🕐 Tue–Sat 11:30am–10pm · Sun closed
Vibe:
Loud
A small but beloved fish spot in the flea market, founded by Bilal Haga from Sakhnin and run with visible pride. The concept is Arab-European fusion: fish & chips with harissa and preserved lemon, sashimi-style sea bream on matbucha with eggplant, ceviche with seasonal fruit, fish shawarma. Everything is fresh and made with care. Open-air courtyard tables face the market square and its weekend street performances. A great early-evening option before moving on elsewhere.

🎉 HH: 20% off, Mon–Thu 5–7pm

🐟 Fish & seafood
🌍 Arab-European

Jaffa border

רומנו

Romano · Derech Jaffa 9
🕐 Daily from 6pm
Vibe:
Loud
The most quintessentially Tel Aviv experience on the list — a buzzing Eyal Shani restaurant-bar in an open historic courtyard, where new chef Pasha Chkin has just refreshed the menu toward a gastropub direction: beef kebab on warm laffa, black pasta with cuttlefish, scallop liver, charred corn, roasted artichoke fritters. Electric energy, beautiful crowd, and Teder — one of the city's best music venues — right next door. The 20% happy hour (until 7:30pm Sun–Thu) makes it a brilliant entry point to the evening.
📋 Menu on Ontopo
GF: most vegetable/fish dishes naturally GF; bread/pasta items are not DF: mostly DF — Eyal Shani style avoids dairy by default
🎉 HH: 20% off, Sun–Thu until 7:30pm
💰 Affordable ₪₪
🎵 Teder next door
⚡ High energy

Recommendations by mood: For the conversation-first evening, start at Beit Kandinof or Beccafico — both are genuinely beautiful and quiet. For the classic Tel Aviv night out, Romano during happy hour then walk next door to Teder is hard to beat. Cicchetti and Vermuteria are the safest choices for a mixed-age group — consistent, reliably good, easy. Shila is the pick if someone wants a proper wow dinner.

For out-of-town guests: Beit Kandinof (unique Jaffa setting), Romano (quintessential TLV energy), or Cicchetti (universally accessible) are all strong first-impression picks.
